About Time Talks

Bringing timepieces
and people together.

Time Talks is an independent platform created to make the world of fine watches more accessible, transparent and enjoyable.

Our speakers cut through hype and jargon to explore how watches are made, why certain models matter, what affects value, and how to buy for the right reasons. The aim is not to sell a particular brand. It is to help people understand what they are buying — and why they love it.
